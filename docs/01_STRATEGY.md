## Business positioning
Independent car body repair shop, 20 years at the bench. Positioned as the local craftsman who makes damage disappear — not a chain, not a valeting outfit. Premium finish, working-shop honesty.

## Target audience
One primary persona: **the stressed phone-user.** They've just scraped a door, been rear-ended, or found a dent in a car park. They're on mobile (assume 80%+ of traffic), searching "car body repair near me" in Edgware/Burnt Oak, and they need three answers in ten seconds:
1. Can these people fix my problem?
2. Are they any good?
3. How do I get a price?

## Key messaging
- Core promise: **"The damage was never there."** (hero headline — locked)
- Proof pillars: 20 years' experience · all work guaranteed · MOT-approved badge · real workshop photos · real Google reviews
- Voice: confident, human, written for the trade. No filler, no corporate padding.

## Conversion strategy
- **Primary CTA: WhatsApp a photo of the damage.** Lower commitment than a call, works out of hours, starts the quote conversation instantly. Biggest element on screen.
- **Secondary CTA: Call the shop.** Ghost/outline button beside primary.
- CTA never leaves the screen: sticky header CTA on desktop, sticky bottom action bar on mobile (Call / WhatsApp / Directions).
- No forms, no newsletter, no "learn more" dead ends. Two actions only.
- Quote-only pricing model — no prices on site.

## Competitors (local context, verified 05 Jul 2026)
Edgware/Burnt Oak body repair search results are dominated by directory listings (Yell, Cylex), mobile-repair franchises, and recovery firms with template sites. No local competitor has a genuinely designed website. A premium single-pager wins this postcode on sight — the bar is on the floor. Sparks' own Google presence is thin (source of the tester opportunity and of the GBP work already in motion).

## Named creative concept
**"THE FINISH."** Everything a body shop sells reduces to one thing: the flawless finish. The site feels like fresh paint under booth lights — deep, dark, glossy, with one hit of spark orange. Restraint is the premium move.

## Success criteria for this build (it's a tester to win the client)
- Client opens it on his phone via WhatsApp link and it looks £20k, loads instantly, nothing broken.
- Every fact on the page is either verified or visibly generic — nothing he can call out as wrong.
- One scroll tells the whole story: promise → services → proof → reviews → contact.
