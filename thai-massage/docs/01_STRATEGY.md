## 2. BUSINESS POSITIONING

Family-run Thai massage studio in Collier Row, Romford — Nid & her team. Genuine Thai practitioners, a real dedicated treatment space, 4.8★ across 241 Google reviews, and a menu organised by **customer-chosen pressure** (Light/Medium, Deep, Your Choice). No competitor sells that clearly.

## 7. NAMED CREATIVE CONCEPT — "THE SLOW HOUR"

Their product is literally time: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 90 minutes, 2 hours. The concept sells the hour itself — the one part of the customer's week where time slows down.

- Headline territory: *"An hour that belongs to you."* / *"Slow down. We'll do the rest."*
- The price list becomes a **duration matrix** — time as the organising axis.
- Motion language: slow, weighty, unhurried (specified in Phase 4).
- Imagery: macro and atmospheric — oil, steam, candlelight, Thai silk. Stillness with one slowly moving element.
